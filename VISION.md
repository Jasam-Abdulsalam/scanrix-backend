# Scanrix — Product Vision

## The Vision

Scanrix is a mobile app that helps health-conscious people instantly understand
whether a food or cosmetic product is actually good for them — before they buy it.

**The problem it solves:** Most people cannot read an ingredient list and know if
a product is safe. They don't know which chemicals are harmful, whether something
suits their skin type, or whether a "healthy-looking" food product is actually high
in things they should avoid. Researching this manually (typing ingredients into
Google) is slow and confusing.

**The solution:** Scan a barcode or the ingredient list on any food or cosmetic
product → get an instant, personalized safety score and plain-English breakdown →
see better alternatives at a similar price if the product isn't great.

**Tagline:** "Scan Smart, Live Better"

## Target Users

Health-conscious consumers (25–45), people with allergies/sensitivities, parents
buying for family, skincare enthusiasts, anyone who shops groceries and wants to
avoid harmful ingredients.
